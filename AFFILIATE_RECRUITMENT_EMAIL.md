## SUBJECT LINE PERFORMANCE TIPS

- **"You're invited to..."** → creates exclusivity, use for warm audience
- **Numbers in subject** → "Earn $81/year per referral" — specific and compelling
- **Personalization** → "[First Name], your affiliate link is ready" for post-signup email
- **Avoid**: "Free," "Act now," excessive emoji — these trigger spam filters
