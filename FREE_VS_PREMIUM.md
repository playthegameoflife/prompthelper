# Free vs Premium - Simple Two-Tier System

## 🆓 FREE TIER

**Price: $0/month**

### Features:
- ✅ **10 prompt enhancements per week**
- ✅ **5 "Ask" questions per week**
- ✅ Basic style templates (default, concise, detailed)
- ✅ View last 1 history item
- ✅ ✨ button in AI chats
- ✅ Keyboard shortcuts (`Ctrl+Shift+E`)
- ✅ Community support

### Your Cost Per Free User:
- **~$0.02/month** (2 cents)
- Most users stay within limits
- Very low cost for user acquisition

---

## ⭐ PREMIUM TIER

**Price: $19.99/month**

### Features:
- ✅ **Unlimited** prompt enhancements
- ✅ **Unlimited** "Ask" questions
- ✅ All style templates (including advanced)
- ✅ Unlimited history (50+ items)
- ✅ Export history (CSV/JSON)
- ✅ Priority API access
- ✅ Priority support
- ✅ Unlimited custom styles

### Your Cost Per Premium User:
- **~$1.00/month** (average API usage)
- Light users: ~$0.50/month
- Heavy users: ~$2.00/month

### Your Profit Per Premium User:
- Revenue: $19.99/month
- API Cost: ~$1.00/month
- Stripe Fee: ~$0.58/month (2.9% + $0.30)
- **Net Profit: ~$18.41/month** 🎉

---

## 💰 Cost Breakdown

### API Costs (Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite):
- Input: $0.10 per 1 million tokens
- Output: $0.40 per 1 million tokens
- **Cost per enhancement: ~$0.00012** (0.012 cents)

### Free User Usage:
- 10 enhancements/week × 4.3 weeks = 43/month
- 5 questions/week × 4.3 weeks = 22/month
- **Total cost: ~$0.007/month**

### Premium User Usage (Average):
- 50 enhancements/month
- **Total cost: ~$0.60/month**

---

## 📊 Summary

| Tier | Price | Your Cost | Your Profit |
|------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Free** | $0 | ~$0.007/month | -$0.007/month |
| **Premium** | $19.99 | ~$1.00/month | **+$18.41/month** |

**Bottom Line:**
- Free users cost you almost nothing (~7 cents/month)
- Premium users are highly profitable (~$18.41 profit/month)
- Very sustainable business model! ✅
