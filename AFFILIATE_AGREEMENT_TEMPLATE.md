## SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Affiliate"** means the individual or entity identified above who has been approved to participate in the Prompt Helper Gemini Affiliate Program.

**1.2 "Affiliate Link"** means a unique tracking URL provided by Company that identifies Affiliate as the source of a referral.

**1.3 "Referred Customer"** means any person who purchases a paid subscription to the Product via Affiliate's Affiliate Link.

**1.4 "Commission"** means the percentage of Net Revenue payable to Affiliate under this Agreement.

**1.5 "Net Revenue"** means all gross payments received by Company from Referred Customers, minus: (a) Stripe or payment processor transaction fees; (b) refunds and chargebacks; and (c) applicable taxes.

**1.6 "Cookie Window"** means the 90-day period following a click on an Affiliate Link during which a purchase by the same user will be attributed to Affiliate.

## SECTION 2 — COMMISSIONS

**2.1 Commission Rate.** Company shall pay Affiliate a commission equal to **30% (thirty percent)** of Net Revenue received from all payments made by Referred Customers.

**2.2 Recurring Nature.** Commissions apply to:
- (a) Initial subscription payments
- (b) All subscription renewal payments
- (c) All upgrades, downgrades, and add-on purchases
- (d) For the full lifetime of each Referred Customer's account

**2.3 Attribution.** A Referred Customer shall be attributed to Affiliate if:
- (a) The customer clicked Affiliate's Affiliate Link within the Cookie Window immediately prior to purchase, OR
- (b) The customer signs up using Affiliate's unique referral code and the purchase occurs within 90 days of the last interaction

**2.4 Credit Priority.** If a customer has multiple potential attributions, credit shall be given to the most recent valid Affiliate Link click. Self-referrals (Affiliate referring themselves) are not eligible for commission.

**2.5 Commission Exclusions.** No commission shall be paid for:
- (a) Free tier signups where no payment is made
- (b) Refunds, chargebacks, or disputed transactions
- (c) Customers identified as fraudulent or duplicates
- (d) Customers acquired through prohibited methods (see Section 5)
