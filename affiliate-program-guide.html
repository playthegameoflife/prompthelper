01 — Commission Structure

The right commission model determines whether affiliates promote you once or for years. Here's the data-driven recommendation.

Recommendation: 30% recurring commission. Affiliates earn 30% of every payment — including renewals — for the full lifetime of the customer they refer. This is the industry standard for SaaS Chrome extensions and creates genuine alignment between your affiliate and product growth.

Why 30% Recurring?

For a freemium-to-premium product with Stripe billing, 30% is the sweet spot. You keep 70% of revenue to cover hosting, support, and operations. Your affiliate earns ongoing passive income — so they stay motivated to keep sending quality referrals. Win-win.

What this means in practice

If a customer pays $9/month via Stripe: affiliate earns $2.70/month as long as that customer stays subscribed. A $99/year plan = $29.70/year per subscriber. 10 paying referrals = $297/month in passive affiliate income.

Cookie & Attribution

Set a 90-day cookie window. If a user clicks an affiliate link and subscribes within 90 days, the affiliate gets credit — even if they come back later. This dramatically increases conversions and reduces affiliate frustration.

03 — Step-by-Step Setup

Detailed setup for all three platforms.

1 Rewardful Setup

  1. 1

    Create Rewardful account

    Go to getrewardful.com → Sign up. Connect your Stripe account via OAuth. This links Rewardful to your Stripe and enables commission tracking.

  2. 2

    Configure commission rate

    In Settings → Payout → Default Commission, set:
    Rate: 30%
    Type: Recurring
    Duration: Unlimited (lifetime)

  3. 3

    Set cookie window

    Settings → Referral → Cookie Window → set to 90 days. This is critical for accurate attribution.

  4. 4

    Create your affiliate portal

    Rewardful auto-generates a portal at yourproduct.rewardful.com. Customize branding, add your own copy, and enable self-serve signup.

  5. 5

    Add your Stripe webhook

    Rewardful provides a webhook URL. In Stripe Dashboard → Webhooks, add the Rewardful endpoint. This syncs all payments and refunds automatically.

  6. 6

    Test with a pilot affiliate

    Create a test affiliate account. Complete a purchase through their link. Verify it appears in Rewardful's dashboard within minutes. Fix any tracking gaps before going live.

2 Gumroad Setup

  1. 1

    Enable Gumroad's affiliate feature

    In Gumroad → Settings → Affiliates → toggle Enable affiliates. You can now onboard affiliates directly within Gumroad.

  2. 2

    Set commission rate

    Set default to 30% recurring. In Advanced settings, choose "For all billing intervals" for lifetime recurring commissions.

  3. 3

    Set up your product

    Add your premium tier as a product or subscription. If using Stripe directly alongside Gumroad, use Gumroad's overlay checkout — this ensures affiliate attribution works.

  4. 4

    Invite affiliates

    Gumroad generates unique affiliate links. Go to your product → Affiliates → Generate link. Share the link with recruits via email or Gumroad's built-in share tools.

  5. 5

    Configure payouts

    Gumroad pays affiliates via PayPal or Stripe Connect. Affiliates complete W-9 forms directly in Gumroad. Set minimum payout threshold (recommended: $25).

3 Custom DIY Setup

  1. 1

    Generate referral links

    Create a URL structure: yourdomain.com/?ref=AFFILIATE_ID or use path-based: yourdomain.com/ref/AFFILIATE_ID. Store affiliate IDs in your database or a spreadsheet.

  2. 2

    Track with Google Analytics 4

    Set up a custom UTM parameter: utm_source=affiliate&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=NAME. Use GA4 Audiences to segment affiliate traffic and set up conversion goals.

  3. 3

    Add Stripe webhook listener

    // Example Stripe webhook handler (Node.js) app.post('/webhook/stripe', (req, res) => { const sig = req.headers['stripe-signature']; const event = stripe.webhooks.constructEvent( req.body, sig, 'WEBHOOK_SECRET' ); if (event.type === 'invoice.paid') { const customer = event.data.object.customer; // Look up affiliate by customer metadata, pay commission await calculateAndPayAffiliate(customer); } });
  4. 4

    Manage affiliates in a spreadsheet

    Track: affiliate name, email, ref code, referred customers, commission earned, payout status. Use Google Sheets + Apps Script for automated commission calculation.

  5. 5

    Manual or PayPal payouts

    Pay affiliates monthly via PayPal or bank transfer. Track W-9 status in your spreadsheet. Set a minimum payout threshold (e.g., $25).

04 — Affiliate Agreement Template

Every affiliate must agree to these terms before joining. Key clauses to include in your affiliate agreement.

§1 — Definitions

"Affiliate" means you (the independent promoter). "Commission" is the percentage of Net Revenue paid to you. "Referred Customer" is anyone who purchases via your unique affiliate link. "Net Revenue" is total payments minus Stripe fees and refunds.

§2 — Commission Rate

  • 30% of Net Revenue for all payments made by Referred Customers
  • Commissions apply to initial subscriptions and all renewals, upgrades, and add-ons
  • Commissions are paid for the full lifetime of the Referred Customer account
  • Cookie window: 90 days from last click

§3 — Commission Exclusions

No commission is paid on: free tier signups (no payment), refunds or chargebacks, self-referrals (you cannot refer yourself), or customers from fraudulent/duplicated clicks. If a customer signs up through multiple affiliates, credit goes to the most recent click.

§4 — Payment Terms

Payouts are processed monthly, within 15 days of month-end. Minimum payout threshold: $25. Payments are sent via PayPal or Stripe Connect. Affiliates are responsible for their own tax obligations. 1099 forms issued when required by US law.

§5 — Permitted Promotional Methods

Allowed: content marketing (blogs, YouTube, social media), email marketing to your own list, paid advertising where permitted, product reviews. All promotional content must be truthful and not misleading.

§6 — Prohibited Conduct

  • No using our brand name or trademarks in misleading ways
  • No spam or unsolicited bulk email (CAN-SPAM / GDPR compliant only)
  • No fake reviews, testimonials, or fabricated claims about the product
  • No cookie stuffing, typosquatting, or domain squatting
  • No using paid search ads on branded terms without written approval
  • No incentive-only offers (bribing people to sign up)

§7 — Termination

Either party may terminate with 7 days notice. Prompt Helper Gemini may terminate immediately for breach of this agreement or suspected fraud. Upon termination, unpaid commissions are paid in the next cycle. Fraudulently earned commissions are never paid and may be reclaimed.

§8 — Relationship

Affiliates are independent contractors, not employees, partners, or agents. You are solely responsible for your taxes, insurance, and legal compliance. You may not represent yourself as an employee or agent of Prompt Helper Gemini.

Legal note: This is a framework template. Have a lawyer review it before going live, especially if you're operating internationally or handling tax forms. Rewardful and Gumroad include compliant agreement templates as part of their platform — use those as your baseline.
